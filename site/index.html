1 · The Mbps reality check

Internet speed is a table size, not a badge. It only matters how many people are sitting down at the same time.

Jargon in 20 seconds

  • Mbps — lanes on a highway; higher = more at once.
  • Download — coming to you (streams, browsing, updates).
  • Upload — going out (video calls, backups). The quiet number that matters more every year.
  • Lag (ping) — round-trip time. Games and calls care more about this than raw speed.

Sweet spots by household

HouseholdSweet spot (down / up)
1 person100–300 / 10–20 Mbps
2 adults200–400 / 20–50 Mbps
Family of 3–4400–500 / 40–100 Mbps
5+ people500–1000 / 100+ Mbps
Heavy uploaders & streamersBuy upload; fiber wins
Run three speed tests (morning, 8 pm peak, late night) and compare against what you pay for. Fast near the router but slow in the bedroom? That's coverage, not bandwidth — see section 3.

2 · Promo pricing & contracts, decoded

The only pricing question that matters: how much does this cost twelve and twenty-four months from now?

Compute the honest average (Avg24)

LayerTypical hazard
Intro priceExpires in 6–24 months
Standard priceOften $10–$40/mo higher
Equipment fee~$5–$15/mo; $0 if you own
Install / activation~$0–$100, often waivable
Contract ETF~$10+/mo remaining

Avg24 = (12 × promo total + 12 × standard total + one-time fees) ÷ 24. A "$49.99" ad is frequently a ~$75 life. Still possibly fine — you just deserve to know it at the register.

Ask any rep, word-for-word: "Monthly price for each of the first 24 months? Equipment fee — can I use my own? One-time fees? Contract or ETF? Any surcharges for my address? Is any price conditional on autopay or bundling?" If they can't answer, that's an answer.

3 · Mesh vs router vs rental

The plan you pay for is a promise; the box is the delivery.

Your homeRight box
Apartment / condo under ~1,500 sq ftSingle good router (or gateway)
House 1,500–3,000 sq ft with dead cornersRouter + one mesh node / 2-pack mesh
3,000+ sq ft, multi-floor, thick wallsMesh, 3+ nodes

Rent (~$60–$180/yr forever): ease and free replacement — fine if you move soon. Own a mid-tier router ($80–$150): pays for itself in 1–2 years. Own a mesh: when coverage says so and you'll stay 2+ years. If you keep the rental gateway anyway, ask for bridge mode and plug in your own router.

Cheap 25-minute fix first: router centered, elevated, out of cabinets, away from the microwave. One antenna vertical, one horizontal. Re-test before buying anything mesh-shaped.

4 · Lower-cost plans

  1. Ask your current provider for current-customer offers — the "cancel-then-win" move (scripts in section 5).
  2. Drop the speed tier on purpose to the number Guide 1 says you need.
  3. Un-bundle. Standalone internet + only the streaming services you actually open wins more often than TV bundles.
  4. Buy the equipment (see section 3's math).
  5. Check low-income / lifeline-style programs if income or circumstances qualify — providers run their own tiers; programs and eligibility change, so check their official pages.
  6. Prepaid plans: no contract, no teaser, one honest total. Great for irregular incomes.

The final tie-breaker in one breath: which plan's Avg24 is lower, which has more upload, which has fewer fees, which am I less afraid of calling? Choose, and close 37 tabs.

5 · Cancel scripts

Clean cancel (no negotiation wanted)

"Hi — calling to cancel my internet service effective [date]. I'm not looking to discuss offers; I've settled on a new provider. Please process the cancellation, confirm no unreturned-equipment charges after return, and give me a confirmation number and the return address."

Cancel-or-discount (the two-way door)

"My bill goes from [$X] this month to [$Y] after the promo ends. I'm planning to switch; I wanted to give you the chance to keep me. If you can get my monthly total — equipment included — near [$walk-away number], I'll happily stay. Otherwise I'd like to cancel at the end of this billing cycle." Then wait through the silence. Offered a slower tier? "That's a slower plan than I'll take — is there a price for the tier I have?" Offered a 6-month teaser? "What can you do that includes month 25?"

Whenever anything is promised

Get a confirmation number — phone, chat, email, always. Return equipment with a receipt or tracking photo, and calendar a check of the final bill ~40 days after canceling.
